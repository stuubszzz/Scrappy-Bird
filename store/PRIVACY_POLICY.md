# Scrappy Bird – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 5 September 2026

Scrappy Bird is an offline arcade game. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- The game runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests.
- Your best score is saved locally on your device only and is never uploaded.
- No accounts, analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking are used.
- No permissions beyond those required by the Android runtime are requested.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address and the effective date above will be updated.
