**App name:** Scrappy Bird
**Short description (80 chars max):**
Flappy Bird, except your bird keeps losing wings. Same rules. Far less control.

**Full description:**
Scrappy Bird starts as the arcade classic you know: tap to flap, thread the pipes, do not touch anything.

Then the bird gets scrappy.

The longer you survive, the more your bird falls apart. Lose a wing and every flap becomes uneven: it drifts sideways, rolls, and refuses to hold a straight line. Lose both and you are flapping with tail feathers alone, where some taps barely lift you and others fling you at the ceiling.

Same physics, same pipes, same one-tap control. Only the bird changes. How far can you get with a bird that will not fly straight?

- Four birds, four worlds:
  - Birdie, the original, dodging pipes over the classic skyline
  - Trumpet, fist-pumping past the White House and dodging a big beautiful border wall
  - Sammich, a banh mi with lettuce wings, weaving between bamboo steamer stacks on a Saigon street at sunset
  - Persu, a blue-and-white bird dodging jail bars on the way to Parliament, where random scandals in the flock wreck its flying
- Faithful Flappy Bird feel: identical gravity, flap strength, pipe gap and speed
- Progressive wing loss at score 8 and 18, each with its own handling
- Feather bursts, screen shake and a bandaged, patched-up bird that shows its damage
- Best score saved on device
- Fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no data collection
